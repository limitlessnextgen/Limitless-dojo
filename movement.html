The manifesto

Where you start does not decide how far you can go.

I Am Limitless is for people refusing to be defined by injury, circumstance, background, gender or access. It is a belief made visible through action: train, rebuild, create opportunity and bring others with you.

Limitless is not the absence of barriers. It is the decision that barriers will not have the final word.

The founder

Built through lived experience.

Danielle Askari is a British coach, athlete and community organiser who has lived in Morocco for more than a decade.

After serious knee problems changed the way she could train, the words I Am Limitless became a private reminder not to surrender her identity. With 500 MAD of second-hand mats on the roof of her home, that message gained a physical home.

The rooftop grew into Go Limitless: martial arts, women’s strength, independent competitions, athlete opportunity and grassroots youth access under one founder-led ecosystem.

The global vision

Rooted locally. Relevant everywhere.

The long-term goal is to make I Am Limitless a recognised symbol of resilience, access and opportunity through sport.

The movement will grow by documenting real transformation, building accountable programmes, forming values-aligned partnerships and creating platforms where overlooked athletes and communities can be seen.

Global recognition is not the goal for its own sake. Recognition is valuable when it attracts resources, opens doors and creates more opportunities for the people the movement exists to serve.

“Build something real locally. Then give the world a reason to care.”
