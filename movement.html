The manifesto

Where you start does not decide how far you can go.

I Am Limitless is for people refusing to be defined by injury, circumstance, background, gender or access. It is a belief made visible through action: train, rebuild, create opportunity and bring others with you.

Limitless is not the absence of barriers. It is the decision that barriers will not have the final word.

How it began

Built through lived experience.

Danielle Askari is a British coach, athlete and community organiser who has lived in Morocco for more than a decade.

After serious knee problems changed the way she could train, the words I Am Limitless became a reminder not to surrender her identity. With 500 MAD of second-hand mats on the roof of her home, that message gained a physical home.

The rooftop grew into Go Limitless: a place for martial arts, strength, coaching, community events and grassroots opportunity in Tamraght.
