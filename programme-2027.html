The funding priority

One flagship programme. Three connected pathways.

The next business-development priority is to give sponsors and grant partners one clear programme to understand, fund and evaluate.

The I Am Limitless Access Programme brings together the strongest proven areas of the movement: women’s access, youth development and athlete opportunity. It is designed to turn grassroots activity into a repeatable twelve-month programme with clear responsibilities, safeguarding, evidence and reporting.

Proposal status: This programme is a funding and partnership concept. It is not presented as already funded or launched. Final participant targets, budget, delivery dates, staffing and legal arrangements will be confirmed with the selected partner before delivery begins.

Proposed programme framework

Three workstreams. One accountable system.

The final scope can be adapted to the funding partner while protecting the programme’s core purpose.

Workstream 01

Women’s Access

Subsidised or free strength, movement and martial arts opportunities designed to reduce cost and confidence barriers.

Proposed delivery: regular sessions, introductory workshops, community experiences and supported progression.

Workstream 02

Youth Pathway

Structured sport, guidance, challenges and leadership experiences for young people in Tamraght.

Proposed delivery: regular sessions, attendance support, equipment, learning experiences and leadership opportunities.

Workstream 03

Athlete Opportunity

Practical support helping selected emerging athletes train, compete and become more visible.

Proposed delivery: competition access, equipment, coaching support, media assets and documented progression.

How success will be measured

Evidence that respects the people behind the numbers.

The programme will combine responsible human stories with agreed quantitative and qualitative evidence.

01

Participation

Unique registered participants, attendance, sessions delivered and programme completion.

02

Retention

Continued attendance, repeat participation and progression into further opportunities.

03

Confidence & Skills

Participant feedback, coach observations and agreed baseline and follow-up questions.

04

Leadership

Young people and participants taking responsibility, supporting others or progressing into leadership roles.

05

Athlete Progression

Training consistency, competition access, performances, visibility and next-step opportunities.

06

Partner Reporting

Agreed quarterly updates covering delivery, finance, learning, risks and responsible stories.

Proposed budget framework

Every funded cost connected to delivery.

A full itemised budget will be developed once programme scale, partner requirements and delivery dates are agreed.

  • Coaching and programme delivery
  • Venue access and safe equipment
  • Participant transport and access support
  • Safeguarding, risk, insurance and consent systems
  • Workshops, learning and leadership activities
  • Athlete competition and development costs
  • Monitoring, evaluation and partner reporting
  • Responsible photography and programme documentation
  • Administration and agreed contingency

Impact definitions

Clear counting. No inflated claims.

Current 500+ community figure: this refers to the broader number of people connected through training, community activity and events. It should not be interpreted as 500 unique participants in one funded programme.

Future programme reporting: funded programme participants will be counted through consented registration and attendance records, with unique participants separated from total attendances.

Events and sponsorships: competition delivery, event attendance and athlete sponsorship will be recorded separately from regular programme participation.
