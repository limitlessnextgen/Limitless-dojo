One movement · Many locations

Rooted in Tamraght. Built for Morocco.

Limitless Dojo is our training base in Tamraght. Go Limitless is the wider movement created to bring people together wherever we can train, compete, learn or meet.

Sometimes it is a major beach wrestling competition. Sometimes it is sunset Muay Thai, sandboarding, a Tap Me Out challenge, a free open mat or a spontaneous session with a few roll mats and whoever is nearby.

The online home of the movement

What the Discord is for.

The Go Limitless Discord is where the wider community connects, discovers what is happening and helps create the next event.

It is open to locals, Moroccan athletes, coaches, nomads, visitors, international members and anyone interested in fitness or martial arts.
