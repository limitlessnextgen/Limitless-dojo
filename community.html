Locally built · Community led

More than a dojo. A movement.

Limitless Dojo is our training base in Tamraght. Go Limitless is the wider movement creating access, confidence, visibility and connection through sport.

The work happens in classes, on rooftops, at the beach, in local gyms and anywhere people are willing to come together. Each project is built around participation rather than perfection.

01

Women’s Access

Supportive strength and movement spaces where women can build confidence, consistency and physical capability together.

02

Youth Pathways

Free guided sport and development opportunities designed to help young people move, learn, lead and see what may be possible.

03

Athlete Opportunity

Coaching, competition support, visibility and practical opportunities for emerging athletes who are ready to work and grow.

04

Locally Produced Events

Beach wrestling, grappling, open mats, challenges and community activations created locally and opened to a wider audience.
