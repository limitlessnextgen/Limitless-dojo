What Limitless Next Gen does

Where someone starts should not decide how far they can go.

Limitless Next Gen creates free and low-cost opportunities through sport, mentorship, events and community.

The work began in Tamraght with outdoor calisthenics, fitness and community movement. It now includes youth training, women’s access, athlete support and independent competitions that help people build confidence, skills and visibility.

Transparency: Limitless Next Gen is currently a grassroots community initiative while its formal nonprofit structure is developed. Partner-funded projects are agreed clearly, with defined budgets, delivery responsibilities and reporting.

Programme areas

Three pathways. One purpose.

Each programme removes a different barrier: access to training, confidence to take part, or support to compete and progress.

Youth Pathway

A safe place to train, belong and lead.

Free martial arts, movement, challenges and leadership experiences that build confidence, discipline and connection.

Support can provide: coaching, safe equipment, transport, workshops, safeguarding and programme evaluation.

Women’s Access

Strength, confidence and community.

Supportive strength, movement and martial arts experiences that make taking part feel safe, welcoming and possible.

Support can provide: subsidised places, coaching, equipment, workshops and community events.

Support women’s access

Athlete Opportunity

Compete. Get seen. Create opportunity.

Practical support that helps emerging athletes prepare, compete, travel and tell their stories responsibly.

Support can provide: entry fees, travel, equipment, medical requirements, coaching and media.

Limitless Youth Project

A place to train. A reason to believe.

Free martial arts, movement and community experiences help young people build confidence, discipline and belonging.

The goal is simple: give young people somewhere positive to show up, learn, take responsibility and discover what they are capable of.

As the programme grows, Limitless Next Gen is formalising safeguarding, consent, attendance and progress tracking so every participant is protected and every partner can see what their support delivers.

How impact is measured

Real stories. Clear evidence.

The human impact matters. So do the systems that protect participants and show partners what changed.

01

Participation

Record who takes part, how often sessions run and whether people stay engaged.

02

Progress

Track confidence, skills, retention, leadership opportunities and participant feedback.

03

Protection

Use clear safeguarding, consent, image-use, risk and referral procedures.

04

Financial Clarity

Agree the budget, responsibilities and evidence before funding begins.

05

Partner Updates

Share agreed outputs, learning and responsible participant stories.

06

Keep Improving

Use data and community feedback to strengthen the next programme.
