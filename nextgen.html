Two years in Tamraght

A grassroots pathway built step by step.

Limitless Next Gen is the non-profit arm of Go Limitless, created to make sport, education and opportunity accessible without cost being a barrier.

The work began two years ago in Tamraght through calisthenics, fitness and free community movement. Those first sessions built trust, confidence and a local foundation.

From there, the pathway grew into a youth project, women’s access, athlete support and the Limitless Beach Wrestling and Grappling competitions.

Limitless Youth Project

Free sport. Real guidance. A place to grow.

The youth project creates free opportunities for young people in Tamraght through martial arts, movement, education and community experiences.

Young people are encouraged to train, learn, take on new challenges and develop confidence, discipline, leadership and a genuine sense of belonging.

The belief

Where someone starts should not decide how far they can go.

Opportunity grows when a young person has somewhere to belong, someone to guide them and a reason to believe in their own potential.

Limitless Next Gen is building that pathway locally, one session, one event and one person at a time.
