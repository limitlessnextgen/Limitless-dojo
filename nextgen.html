What Limitless Next Gen is

A grassroots pathway built step by step.

Limitless Next Gen is the community-impact initiative within Go Limitless, created to make sport, guidance and opportunity more accessible where cost or circumstance can be a barrier.

The work began in Tamraght through free calisthenics, fitness and community movement. It has expanded into youth development, women’s access, athlete support and independent competitions.

Transparency: Limitless Next Gen is developing its formal nonprofit structure. Until that process is complete, it is presented accurately as a grassroots community initiative. Project funding, delivery responsibilities and reporting expectations are agreed directly with each partner.

Limitless Youth Project

Confidence grows when someone has somewhere to belong.

The youth pathway creates free opportunities through martial arts, movement, education and community experiences.

Young people are encouraged to train, learn, take on new challenges and develop confidence, discipline, leadership and a sense of belonging.

The next development goal is to formalise a repeatable programme structure with participant consent, safeguarding procedures, attendance records, outcome measures and partner reporting.

How impact will be evidenced

From powerful stories to accountable programmes.

Partners need both human stories and reliable evidence. The movement is building the systems to provide both.

01

Participation

Record unique participants, attendance, session delivery and programme completion.

02

Progress

Track confidence, skills, retention, leadership opportunities and participant feedback.

03

Protection

Use clear safeguarding, consent, image-use, risk and referral procedures.

04

Financial Clarity

Agree programme budgets, eligible costs, responsibilities and evidence before funding begins.

05

Partner Reporting

Provide agreed updates, outputs, learning and responsible participant stories.

06

Long-Term Learning

Use data and community feedback to improve future programmes rather than only reporting activity.
