Good to know

Can complete beginners join?

Yes. Classes are open to all levels and no previous experience is required.

Is equipment available?

Yes. Equipment is available to use during training.

Where do sessions take place?

Martial arts and rooftop strength take place at Limitless Dojo in Tamraght. Women’s Strength takes place at Nassim Gym in Tamraght.

How do I confirm a class?

Use the class enquiry form and your details will open in WhatsApp, ready to send directly to Go Limitless.
