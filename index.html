Built from the ground up

From 500 MAD of mats to a community.

Danielle Askari is a British coach and athlete who has lived in Morocco for more than a decade.

After serious knee problems changed the way she could train, the words I Am Limitless became a reminder not to surrender her identity. With 500 MAD of second-hand mats on the roof of her home, that message became a place where other people could train and belong.
