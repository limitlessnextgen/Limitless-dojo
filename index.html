I Am Limitless · From message to movement

The rooftop was born.

Danielle Askari is a British coach, athlete and organiser who has lived in Morocco for over 10 years. With over a decade of coaching experience in strength, physique transformation, contest preparation and posing, her own sporting path has taken her from bodybuilding stages to martial arts mats and long distance endurance challenges.

Her journey has also been shaped by serious knee problems and repeated dislocations. They changed how she could train and move, but never took away her identity as an athlete. From that experience came the words I Am Limitless, first as a reminder to herself and then as a movement for anyone refusing to be defined by injury, circumstance or where they started.

That movement needed a home. With 500 MAD of second hand mats laid across the roof of her home in Tamraght, a rooftop with no limits was born. What began as a small place to train grew into Limitless Dojo, women’s classes, open mats, athlete led events and Limitless Next Gen, creating access, visibility and belonging for young people and emerging athletes.

I Am Limitless began as a reminder. The rooftop gave that message a home.

Meet the coaches · Founder & coach

Coach Danielle.

Over a decade in bodybuilding.
Ex competitor. Coach.
Still learning. Still rebuilding.

Bodybuilding is where it started for me, but injury changed everything. I had to rebuild my body through a different way of training: hybrid performance, martial arts, calisthenics, mobility, endurance and strength that actually carries over into life.

That became Limitless.

Now I coach online and in person, helping people build their body, confidence, strength, fitness and mindset with structure that fits real life.

Online coaching includes:

  • Personalised workouts
  • Nutrition guidance
  • Regular check ins
  • Coaching app access
  • Progress and habit tracking
  • Hormone and cycle tracking where needed

Training should work with your body, not against it.

Limitless Next Gen · Youth Project

Free sport. Real guidance. A place to grow.

Good to know

Your questions, answered.

Where is Go Limitless located?

Go Limitless is based in Tamraght, near Agadir, Morocco. Training takes place at the Limitless rooftop dojo.

Can complete beginners join?

Yes. Classes are open to all levels and no previous experience is required.

What classes and coaching are available?

We offer jujutsu, grappling and Muay Thai, strength and conditioning, women’s classes, open mats, workshops, youth development and personalised online coaching.

Do you have equipment to use?

Yes. Equipment is available to use during training.

Are there women’s and youth sessions?

Yes. We run supportive women’s sessions and free development opportunities through Limitless Next Gen.

How do I book or enquire?

Complete the Try a Class form to send your details directly by WhatsApp, or message @limitless_dojo on Instagram.

What is Limitless Next Gen?

Limitless Next Gen is the social impact arm of Go Limitless, creating free sport, education and opportunities for young people, women and emerging athletes.
