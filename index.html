The founder behind the movement

From 500 MAD of mats to a movement.

Danielle Askari is a British coach, athlete and organiser who has lived in Morocco for more than a decade.

Serious knee problems changed her original athletic path. The words I Am Limitless became a reminder not to surrender her identity. With 500 MAD of second-hand mats on the roof of her home, that message became a place—and the place became a community.

Go Limitless now connects martial arts, strength, coaching, events, youth access, women’s access and athlete opportunity under one founder-led ecosystem.
