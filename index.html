Built through lived experience

A message became a movement.

Go Limitless grew from resilience, more than a decade of coaching and the belief that where someone starts should never decide how far they can go.

With 500 MAD of second-hand mats laid across the roof of her home, Dannii created a place to train. The rooftop became a dojo, the dojo built a community, and the community created events and opportunities far beyond the mats.

I Am Limitless began as a reminder. The rooftop gave that message a home.
