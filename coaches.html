Founder & coach

Coach Danielle.

Over a decade in bodybuilding. Ex competitor. Coach. Still learning. Still rebuilding.

Bodybuilding is where it started, but injury changed everything. Danielle rebuilt through hybrid performance, martial arts, calisthenics, mobility, endurance and strength that carries over into life.

Now she coaches online and in person, helping people build their body, confidence, strength, fitness and mindset with structure that fits real life.

  • Personalised training
  • Nutrition guidance
  • Regular check ins
  • Coaching app access
  • Progress and habit tracking
  • Hormone and cycle tracking where needed

Coach Mo.

Coach Mo leads BJJ, Muay Thai and combat sports sessions on the rooftop in Tamraght.

After living in Vietnam and coaching BJJ, MMA, Muay Thai and boxing, Mo also brings experience commentating at combat sports competitions. A long standing martial artist and competitor, he combines technical knowledge with energy and a genuine passion for helping people learn.

  • Gold medallist in contact sports

  • Long standing martial artist
  • Coach and competitor
