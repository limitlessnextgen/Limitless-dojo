Full biography

Danielle Askari is a British coach, athlete and community organiser who has lived in Morocco for more than a decade. After serious knee problems changed her original athletic path, the words I Am Limitless became a personal reminder not to surrender her identity. With 500 MAD of second-hand mats on the roof of her home in Tamraght, she created a place to train. That rooftop grew into Go Limitless: martial arts, women’s strength, coaching, independent competitions, athlete support and grassroots youth access. Her focus is to strengthen locally built opportunity through sport in Morocco.

About Go Limitless.

Go Limitless is a founder-led sport and community platform based in Tamraght, Morocco.

Founded by Danielle Askari, it brings together martial arts, strength, coaching, independent athlete events and grassroots community work. Its purpose is to help people train, rebuild and create opportunity while making local sport more accessible and emerging athletes more visible.
