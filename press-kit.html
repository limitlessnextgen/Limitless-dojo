Full biography

Danielle Askari is a British coach, athlete and community organiser who has lived in Morocco for more than a decade. After serious knee problems changed her original athletic path, the words I Am Limitless became a personal reminder not to surrender her identity. With 500 MAD of second-hand mats on the roof of her home in Tamraght, she created a place to train. That rooftop grew into Go Limitless: martial arts, women’s strength, coaching, independent competitions, athlete sponsorship and grassroots youth access. Danielle is building I Am Limitless as a global movement centred on resilience, access, visibility and opportunity through sport.

About Go Limitless.

Go Limitless is a founder-led sport and community ecosystem based in Tamraght, Morocco.

Founded by Danielle Askari, it brings together Limitless Dojo, strength and online coaching, independent athlete events, the I Am Limitless movement and Limitless Next Gen’s grassroots impact work. Its purpose is to help people train, rebuild and create opportunity while making sport feel more accessible and emerging athletes more visible.
