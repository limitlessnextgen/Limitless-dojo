# Go Limitless Business Development Plan

## Strategic objective

Build Go Limitless into a sustainable founder-led sport and community ecosystem with three connected income routes:

1. **Commercial revenue** — dojo memberships, strength memberships, coaching, private sessions, retreats and events.
2. **Founder revenue** — speaking, brand campaigns, workshops, consulting and media collaborations.
3. **Impact funding** — programme sponsorship, grants, research partnerships, equipment support and athlete-opportunity funding.

The public story is led by Danielle Askari. The commercial umbrella is Go Limitless. I Am Limitless is the movement. Limitless Next Gen is the grassroots impact initiative.

## Flagship funding priority

Lead partnership and grant conversations with one clear proposition:

**I Am Limitless Access Programme 2027**

A proposed twelve-month programme combining:

- Women’s Access
- Youth Pathway
- Athlete Opportunity
- Community activations
- Quarterly partner reporting

Do not present the programme as funded or launched until an agreement exists. Final targets, budget, delivery dates, staffing and legal arrangements must be confirmed before delivery.

## Weekly business-development targets

Every week:

- Research and qualify **10 potential sponsors or brand partners**.
- Research and qualify **5 grant makers, universities or delivery partners**.
- Contact **5 journalists, podcasts, documentary producers or speaking platforms**.
- Contact **5 local tourism, fitness, hospitality or sport partners**.
- Complete at least **5 meaningful follow-ups** from previous outreach.
- Publish at least **one founder-led proof story** showing real delivery, not only inspiration.

Quality matters more than mass outreach. Every contact should have a clear reason for fit.

## Pipeline stages

Track every opportunity through these stages:

1. **Identified** — organisation or media contact found.
2. **Qualified** — mission, audience, geography and budget appear relevant.
3. **Contacted** — personalised message sent.
4. **Engaged** — reply, introduction or meeting secured.
5. **Discovery** — goals, budget, timing and decision process understood.
6. **Proposal** — written scope, deliverables, responsibilities and budget sent.
7. **Negotiation** — terms and reporting agreed.
8. **Won** — signed agreement and delivery plan in place.
9. **Delivered** — activity completed with evidence.
10. **Renewal** — results reviewed and next opportunity proposed.

Record the organisation, contact person, opportunity, source, last action, next action, follow-up date, estimated value and outcome.

## Evidence standards

### Community reach

The current 500+ figure refers to broader people connected through training, community activity and events. Do not describe it as 500 unique participants in a single programme.

### Funded programmes

For future funded activity, record:

- Unique registered participants
- Consent and relevant guardian consent
- Session attendance
- Total sessions delivered
- Completion and retention
- Participant feedback
- Coach observations
- Progression and leadership opportunities
- Safeguarding or risk issues and actions
- Agreed financial evidence

### Events

Record event athletes, spectators, officials, partners, safety provision, media assets and partner benefits separately from programme participation.

### Athlete sponsorship

Record the support provided, agreed responsibilities, competitions entered, progress, media use and end-of-period review.

## Partnership proposal rules

Every proposal should state:

- The problem being addressed
- The proposed intervention
- Target participants
- Location and delivery period
- Outputs and expected outcomes
- Itemised budget
- Responsibilities of each party
- Safeguarding and consent approach
- Evidence and reporting schedule
- Brand or partner benefits
- Payment terms
- Cancellation and risk arrangements

Sell ownership of a meaningful outcome before selling logo placement.

## First 90 days

### Days 1–30

- Finalise the 2027 programme scope and draft budget.
- Create safeguarding, consent, risk and image-use documents.
- Build a list of 50 qualified partners and 30 media contacts.
- Begin founder-led proof content.
- Set up professional domain email.

### Days 31–60

- Hold discovery conversations.
- Send tailored proposals to the strongest opportunities.
- Secure letters of support from local and international partners.
- Improve participant and event evidence records.
- Publish one strong founder or impact story each week.

### Days 61–90

- Aim to close the first programme, event or founding-growth partnership.
- Produce a delivery and reporting calendar.
- Announce only confirmed agreements.
- Create a case study from the first completed partner activation.
- Use the result to approach larger sponsors, media and funders.

**Evidence → outreach → meetings → proposals → partnerships → delivery → documented results → renewal.**
