Founder-led and community-rooted

A credible story. A proven local platform. A bigger opportunity.

Danielle Askari is a British coach, athlete and organiser who has lived in Morocco for more than a decade and built Go Limitless from 500 MAD of second-hand mats on the roof of her home.

Today, the ecosystem connects commercial training, women’s access, youth development, independent competitions, athlete sponsorship and founder-led storytelling.

Current organisational position: Go Limitless operates as the commercial training and events umbrella. Limitless Next Gen is the grassroots community-impact initiative and is developing its formal nonprofit structure. Partnership terms, financial arrangements and reporting are agreed transparently for each project.

Partnership opportunities

Support an outcome, not just a logo.

Each partnership is built around a defined programme, audience, budget, delivery plan and reporting agreement.

Women’s Access Partner

Make training more accessible to women.

Support subsidised or free strength, martial arts and community experiences for local women, including coaching, equipment and programme delivery.

Possible measures: participants, sessions, attendance, retention and participant feedback.

Youth Pathway Partner

Support youth sessions, safe equipment, transport, learning experiences, coach development and structured pathways into sport and leadership.

Athlete Opportunity Partner

Help emerging athletes compete and be seen.

Fund competition fees, travel, equipment, coaching, nutrition support, medical requirements and responsible athlete storytelling.

Limitless Events Partner
