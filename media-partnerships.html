Founder-led and locally rooted

A direct relationship with the person building the work.

Danielle Askari is a British coach, athlete and organiser who has lived in Morocco for more than a decade and built Go Limitless from 500 MAD of second-hand mats on the roof of her home.

Today, Go Limitless connects paid training and coaching with community access, independent events and emerging-athlete support.

Current organisational position: Go Limitless operates as the commercial training and events umbrella. Limitless Next Gen is the grassroots impact initiative and is developing its formal nonprofit structure. Every funded project must have an agreed scope, budget, responsibilities and reporting process.

Three partnership routes

Choose the outcome you want to support.

The strongest partnerships begin with one clear objective rather than a long list of benefits.

01 · Women & Youth Access

Help more people take part.

Support structured access to strength, martial arts, guidance, equipment and community experiences for local women and young people.

Possible measures: unique participants, sessions, attendance, retention and feedback.

View the proposed programme

02 · Athletes & Events

Help athletes compete and be seen.

Support selected athletes or independent events through equipment, entry fees, travel, safety, production and responsible storytelling.

Possible measures: athletes supported, events delivered, performances, media assets and audience engagement.

Explore the event platform

03 · Founding Growth

Strengthen the systems behind the work.

Support documentation, safeguarding development, monitoring, programme preparation and the practical systems needed to grow responsibly.

Best for: a values-aligned organisation seeking a deeper relationship with the founder and the wider platform.
