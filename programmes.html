LIMITLESS HYBRID

Eight weeks. Four phases.

The goal is not to destroy yourself every session. It is to progress, adapt and finish the block more capable than you started.

Weeks 1–2

Build the Base

Movement quality, repeatable strength work, controlled conditioning and technical calisthenics practice.

Weeks 3–4

Build Strength

Progress the main lifts, increase pulling and pushing volume and add harder bodyweight variations.

Weeks 5–6

Build Capacity

Keep the strength work moving while increasing athletic conditioning and work capacity.

Weeks 7–8

Perform

Bring the pieces together, test progress and finish with clear benchmarks for your next block.

Inside the programme

A week that makes sense.

Four purposeful sessions with recovery space between them.

  • Strength progressions
  • Pull-up and calisthenics work
  • Lower and upper body training
  • Conditioning without endless cardio
  • Mobility and warm-up guidance
  • Simple progress tracking
DayFocus
Day 1Lower body strength + push + short conditioning
Day 2Pull strength + calisthenics skill + core
Day 3Athletic conditioning + mobility
Day 4Full-body strength + capacity finisher
