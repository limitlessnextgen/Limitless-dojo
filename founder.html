Founder profile

Building from lived experience.

Danielle Askari is a British coach, athlete and community organiser who has lived in Morocco for more than a decade.

With over a decade of coaching experience across physique transformation, contest preparation, posing, strength and performance, her sporting identity has moved from bodybuilding stages into martial arts, calisthenics and endurance.

Serious knee problems and repeated dislocations changed the way she could train. They did not end her identity as an athlete. Instead, they forced a wider question: what does it mean to keep moving when the original route is no longer available?

I Am Limitless began as a reminder not to surrender my identity. The rooftop turned that reminder into something other people could belong to.

The origin

A message became a movement.

The words I Am Limitless began as a personal statement against being defined by injury, circumstance or what had been lost.

With 500 MAD of second-hand mats laid across the roof of her home in Tamraght, Danielle created a place to train. That rooftop became Limitless Dojo. The dojo built a community. The community created women’s classes, youth access, independent competitions and athlete opportunity.

Go Limitless now connects commercial training and coaching with the wider founder-led mission of access, visibility, resilience and opportunity through sport.

The vision

Build something real locally. Give the world a reason to care.

Danielle’s long-term goal is to make I Am Limitless globally recognised as a movement that turns resilience into access, visibility and opportunity.

Recognition matters when it brings resources to communities, creates platforms for athletes and opens doors that would otherwise remain closed.
