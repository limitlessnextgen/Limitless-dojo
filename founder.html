The beginning

The rooftop was born.

Danielle Askari is a British coach, athlete and organiser who has lived in Morocco for over 10 years.

With over a decade of coaching experience in strength, physique transformation, contest preparation and posing, her sporting path has taken her from bodybuilding stages to martial arts mats, calisthenics and long distance endurance challenges.

Her journey has also been shaped by serious knee problems and repeated dislocations. They changed how she could train and move, but never took away her identity as an athlete.

I Am Limitless began as a reminder. The rooftop gave that message a home.

From injury to identity

A message became a movement.

From that experience came the words I Am Limitless, first as a reminder to herself and then as a movement for anyone refusing to be defined by injury, circumstance or where they started.

That movement needed a home. With 500 MAD of second hand mats laid across the roof of her home in Tamraght, a rooftop with no limits was born.

What began as a small place to train grew into Limitless Dojo, women’s classes, open mats, athlete led events and Limitless Next Gen, creating access, visibility and belonging for young people and emerging athletes.

The athlete and the coach

Every chapter built the next.

Bodybuilding built the foundation. Injury changed the method. Martial arts, mobility, endurance and community gave the work a wider purpose.

The vision

A first female led dojo with community at its core.

Go Limitless is building a home where martial arts, strength and grassroots opportunity sit under one name.

The aim is not simply to run classes. It is to make training feel open, create visible pathways for emerging athletes and show what can be built from the ground up.
