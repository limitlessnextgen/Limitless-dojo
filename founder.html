The founder story

Built through lived experience.

Danielle Askari is a British coach, athlete and community organiser who has lived in Morocco for more than a decade.

Her background began in bodybuilding, physique coaching, posing, strength and performance. Serious knee problems later changed the way she could train and forced her to rebuild her identity as an athlete.

The words I Am Limitless became a personal reminder not to be defined by injury or circumstance. With 500 MAD of second-hand mats on the roof of her home, that message became a place other people could train and belong.

The rooftop did not begin as a large plan. It began as a place to keep moving—and it grew because people needed it.

From rooftop to community

A small beginning became something useful.

Limitless Dojo began on Danielle’s rooftop in Tamraght. Training brought together local residents, women, young people, travellers, coaches and athletes.

From there came strength classes, martial arts, community events, independent beach wrestling and grappling competitions, athlete support and grassroots access work.

Go Limitless now connects paid training and coaching with community opportunity in a way that remains practical, local and founder-led.
