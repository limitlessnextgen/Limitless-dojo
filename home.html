I Am Limitless · From message to movement

Built through lived experience.

Danielle Askari built Go Limitless from a personal message of resilience into a home for training, opportunity and community.

With 500 MAD of second-hand mats laid across the roof of her home, the first version of Limitless Dojo was born.

I Am Limitless began as a reminder. The rooftop gave that message a home.

Impact so far

Rooted locally. Built to grow.
